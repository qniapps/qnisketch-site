Q. What are the recommended system requirements?
  • Minimum: Android 7.0 or higher, 3GB RAM or more
  • Recommended: Android 13 or higher, 4GB RAM or more
  • High-resolution photo processing requires substantial device memory.
    For a smooth and stable experience, devices with 4GB of RAM or higher are recommended.
